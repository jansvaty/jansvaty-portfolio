Case studies

Kiwi Compass

Around 700,000 New Zealanders live in Australia, and most picked their city on hearsay. I designed and built Kiwi Compass end-to-end: an installable web app that matches who you are - work, hobbies, budget, household - against twelve Australian cities using official data, with a self-weighted scoring model, a radar graph of your priorities, and the reasoning behind every score.

PowerAutomated AI Agent

To simplify my team's workflow and take advantage of rapidly emerging AI tools, I built a PowerAutomated agent that streamlines communication between the team administrator and clients - replacing a manual form with an intelligent first point of contact for the Learning Design team at Customs.

Stake: Design System

For this project, I developed a design system for Hello Stake investing broker. The aim was to help Stake streamline their internal and external communication as well as the development process by giving their digital assets a structure.

Sharesies: Investing 101

I led a three-month project for Sharesies focused on discovering and understanding barriers that prospective investors face when starting their investing journey. Our team addressed them by developing an educational hub with targeted resources, a calculation tool and an interactive investing crash course.

Copilot Chat 101

Tasked with creating the first AI learning resource across New Zealand Customs Service, I designed a 20-minute Rise 360 module introducing Copilot Chat to all staff - covering practical use, responsible behaviour, and privacy guidance. The module was later adopted by six other government agencies, as it turned out to be the first learning resource of its kind in New Zealand public sector.
